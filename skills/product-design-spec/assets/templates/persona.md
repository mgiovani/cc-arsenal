# Persona: {{ROLE_LABEL}}

> **No invented demographics.** Do not invent age, gender, income, disability, or geography. Include a
> demographic attribute only when it is backed by research evidence AND it materially changes the design;
> otherwise omit it. A persona is a design tool grounded in evidence, not a fictional character.

**Evidence level**: `research-validated` (from interviews / analytics / usability data) |
`evidence-informed` (from indirect signals) | `proto-persona` (a documented assumption, to be validated).
State which, and cite the source.

**Primary goal / job-to-be-done**: "When {{situation}}, I want to {{motivation}}, so I can {{outcome}}."

**Context of use**: device(s), environment, and frequency (e.g. "mobile, on the move, several times a day").
Evidence-backed, not assumed.

**Key needs / pain points**: the top 2-3, each traceable to a finding or an approved requirement. No
invented frustrations.

**Constraints / accessibility needs**: include ONLY when evidenced (e.g. "operates keyboard-only per
usability session S3"). Never a placeholder disability.

**Traces to**: the PRD requirement id(s) and/or research finding(s) this persona informs. A persona that
drives no requirement doesn't belong in the spec.
