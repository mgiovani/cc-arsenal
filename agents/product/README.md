# Product Agents

Product managers specializing in requirements analysis, story management, and team coordination.

## Purpose

Product agents provide expertise in:
- PRD (Product Requirements Document) creation
- Story management and planning
- Team coordination and communication
- Market research and competitive analysis
- Product strategy and roadmap planning

## Development

Product agents should focus on:
- Gathering and documenting requirements
- Creating clear, actionable user stories
- Facilitating stakeholder communication
- Managing project scope and priorities
- Ensuring alignment with business objectives

## Tools

Common tools for product agents:
- Read, Write, Edit for documentation
- TodoWrite for project tracking
- WebSearch, WebFetch for market research
- Glob, Grep for codebase understanding
